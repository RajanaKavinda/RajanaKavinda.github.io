# Rajana Kavinda Samarakoon

### Robotics Engineer & Researcher

I’m a thesis-based Master of Engineering student at Memorial University of Newfoundland, researching **multi-robot cooperative localization**. My interests include robot state estimation, multi-sensor fusion, autonomous navigation, and ROS2-based robotics.

I completed my bachelor’s degree at the **University of Moratuwa’s Faculty of Engineering**, specializing in **Electronic and Telecommunication Engineering**. My work brings together research and practical development in motion planning, feedback control, computer vision, and edge AI.
